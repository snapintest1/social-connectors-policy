Privacy Policy

Daily Bloom Café — Instagram and Facebook AirSync Connector

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Instagram and Facebook AirSync Connector ("Connector") accesses and processes information from authorized Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages to synchronize supported social interactions.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the enabled features, permissions granted, and information made available by Meta's APIs, the Connector may process:

  • Instagram account IDs, usernames, and available profile information.
  • Instagram post and Reel IDs, captions, links, timestamps, comments, replies, and available commenter information.
  • Supported Instagram messages, conversation information, and associated media attachments, where available.
  • Facebook Page IDs, Page names, and available Page information.
  • Facebook Page post IDs, text, permalinks, timestamps, comments, replies, and available commenter information.
  • Supported Facebook Messenger conversation and message information, including message content, timestamps, and available sender details, where the required permissions are granted.
  • Authentication tokens and connection information needed to maintain authorized access to the connected accounts and Pages.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to provide the requested integration features, including:

  • Synchronizing supported Instagram and Facebook interactions.
  • Organizing supported comments, replies, and messages into configured records or tickets.
  • Providing authorized users with the ability to review and respond to supported social interactions.
  • Maintaining synchronization state, handling errors, and troubleshooting integration issues.
  • Maintaining authorized connections to Meta services.

We do not sell Meta platform data or use it for advertising purposes. Information is processed for the functionality of the Connector and other purposes permitted by applicable Meta platform terms.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Information synchronized by the Connector may be stored and processed and the infrastructure used to operate the integration. Access should be limited to authorized users and services according to applicable permissions and security controls.

Data may be processed by service providers involved in delivering the service, subject to applicable agreements and requirements. We do not sell Meta platform data or share it with third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the integration, subject to applicable retention practices, service configuration, and legal requirements.

Disconnecting an Instagram account or Facebook Page stops or limits future access as applicable, but does not necessarily delete information already synchronized or other authorized storage systems. Such information is handled according to the applicable deletion procedures and retention requirements.

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information processed through this Connector, contact us using the email address below. Please identify the connected Instagram account or Facebook Page and describe the information you want deleted. We will handle valid requests according to our deletion procedures and applicable legal requirements.

Users may revoke application access through the relevant Meta or Instagram settings. Revoking access may stop future access but does not automatically delete information previously synchronized.

We will honor applicable Meta data-deletion requests and platform requirements, including configured data-deletion callbacks where applicable.

7. Security

Appropriate technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the Connector against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Access to authentication credentials and sensitive information should be restricted and protected. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Instagram and Facebook are services provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Access to their data is governed by applicable Meta terms, platform policies, and granted permissions.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Connector, data processing practices, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
