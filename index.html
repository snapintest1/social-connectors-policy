Privacy Policy

Daily Bloom Café — Instagram AirSync Connector for DevRev

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Instagram AirSync Connector ("Connector") accesses and processes Instagram data to synchronize supported social interactions with DevRev.

2. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features and granted permissions, the Connector may process:

  • Instagram account IDs and usernames.
  • Post and Reel IDs, captions, links, and timestamps.
  • Comments, replies, commenter IDs, usernames, and timestamps.
  • Supported Instagram messages and conversation information.
  • Media attachments associated with supported messages, where available.

3. How We Use Information

  • Synchronize supported Instagram interactions with DevRev.
  • Organize comments and messages into configured DevRev records or tickets.
  • Allow authorized users to review and respond to supported interactions.
  • Maintain synchronization state and troubleshoot integration issues.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Information synchronized by the Connector may be stored and processed within DevRev and the infrastructure used to operate the integration. Access should be limited according to applicable permissions and security controls.

Data may be processed by service providers involved in delivering the service, subject to applicable agreements and requirements. Actual storage, retention, and sharing practices depend on the implementation and applicable provider policies.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained according to applicable service configuration, retention practices, and legal requirements. Disconnecting Instagram does not necessarily delete information already synchronized to DevRev.

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of Instagram information processed through this Connector, contact us at the email address below. Identify the connected account and describe the information you want deleted. Requests will be reviewed and handled according to applicable policies and legal requirements.

Users may also revoke application access through relevant Meta or Instagram settings. Revoking access may stop future access but does not automatically delete previously synchronized data.

7. Security

Appropriate technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information processed by the Connector. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Instagram services are provided by Meta. Use of Instagram is subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies. DevRev data processing is subject to applicable DevRev terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Connector, data practices, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
